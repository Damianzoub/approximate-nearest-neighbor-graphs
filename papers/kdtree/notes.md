# KD-Trees: Multidimensional binary search trees used for associative searching

**Author:** Jon Louis Bentley  
**Journal:** Communications of the ACM, Volume 18, Issue 9, Pages 509–517  
**Year:** 1975  
**Publisher:** ACM  
**DOI:** 10.1145/361002.361007

## Where to get the PDF

Not freely available (1975 ACM paper). Access via:
- ACM Digital Library: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/361002.361007
- Institutional access required

## Summary

The foundational paper introducing the k-d tree (k-dimensional binary search tree) data structure for multidimensional associative searching. Proves average-case insertion O(log n), and establishes the basis for spatial data structures used in nearest neighbor search. Works well for low dimensions (d ≤ 20) but degrades to near-exhaustive search in high dimensions due to the curse of dimensionality.
