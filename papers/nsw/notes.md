# NSW: Approximate nearest neighbor algorithm based on navigable small world graphs

**Authors:** Yury Malkov, Alexander Ponomarenko, Andrey Logvinov, Vladimir Krylov  
**Journal:** Information Systems, Volume 45, Pages 61–68  
**Year:** 2014  
**Publisher:** Elsevier  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.is.2013.10.006

## Where to get the PDF

Not freely available on arXiv. Access via:
- Elsevier: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306437913001300
- ResearchGate: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/259126397

## Summary

Original paper introducing the NSW (Navigable Small World) graph algorithm for approximate nearest neighbor search. Constructs a graph with both local and long-range edges using a greedy insertion strategy. Achieves logarithmic search complexity at fixed accuracy. This is the direct predecessor of HNSW (2018).
