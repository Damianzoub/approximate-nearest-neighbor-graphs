# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. AI has evolved from narrow rule-based systems to general-purpose learning machines capable of performing tasks once thought exclusive to humans.

## Deep Learning and Neural Networks

Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with many layers — hence the term "deep" — to model complex patterns in data. Inspired by the structure of the human brain, these networks consist of interconnected nodes (neurons) arranged in layers. The input layer receives raw data, hidden layers extract increasingly abstract features, and the output layer produces predictions or decisions.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) revolutionised image recognition by automatically learning spatial hierarchies of features. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and their successor Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks enabled sequential data modelling, powering early speech recognition and machine translation systems.

## Transformer Architecture and Large Language Models

The Transformer architecture, introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need," fundamentally changed natural language processing. Unlike RNNs, Transformers process entire sequences in parallel using self-attention mechanisms that weigh the relevance of each token relative to all others. This parallelism allowed training on massive datasets, giving rise to Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini.

LLMs are trained on trillions of tokens of text and can perform tasks ranging from code generation and summarisation to complex reasoning and creative writing. Fine-tuning and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) align these models with human preferences and safety requirements.

## AI in Healthcare

AI is transforming healthcare by accelerating drug discovery, improving diagnostic accuracy, and personalising treatment plans. Deep learning models trained on medical imaging datasets can detect cancers, diabetic retinopathy, and cardiovascular conditions with accuracy matching or exceeding specialist clinicians. AlphaFold, developed by DeepMind, solved the protein folding problem — predicting 3D protein structure from amino acid sequences — with transformative implications for biology and medicine.

## Ethical Considerations

As AI systems become more capable, questions of fairness, accountability, transparency, and safety become critical. Bias in training data can lead to discriminatory outcomes. Black-box models make it difficult to understand and audit decisions in high-stakes domains. Researchers are developing explainable AI (XAI) techniques and regulatory frameworks to ensure AI systems are trustworthy and aligned with human values.

## The Future of AI

Emerging research directions include multimodal AI (combining text, image, audio, and video), AI agents that autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks, and neuromorphic computing that mimics biological neural circuits for energy-efficient inference. The long-term goal of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) — systems that can perform any cognitive task a human can — remains actively debated both technically and philosophically.
